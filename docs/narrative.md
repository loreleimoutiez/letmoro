# Narrative guide

## Premise

Moro has endured a difficult past, whose details remain offstage. There is no trauma mystery to solve. With little hope, Moro takes a letter-delivery job to earn a living, gain independence, and build a stable life.

Opening line: **“Cross the street to find your first job…”** This refers to the fictional street. Never ask the player to cross a real road or use real-world GPS instructions.

## Tone

Frequent tranquility, with earned humor, joy, sadness, romance, and drama arising from characters and consequences. Let scenes breathe. Fantastical elements are rare and discreet, gradually making familiar surroundings uncertain. Do not insert emotional shocks or random supernatural incidents simply to vary the mood.

Ordinary milestones matter: a first payment, a room, familiar company, a personal project. Relationships do not magically erase Moro's past.

## Player's life

Building a personal life and choosing projects form the campaign's foundation. Home, independence, friendships, exploration, and professional development must support a full route without political involvement or romance.

Romantic arcs are optional, deliberate, and as developed as political arcs. Friendship has independent value. The player may pursue either, both, or neither. Consent and commitment require an explicit decision; automatic initiatives must not start a romance.

## “M” the Accursed

The territories are ruled by “M” the Accursed. Increasing taxes combine understated satire with concrete local effects. First settlement:

| Item | Coins |
| --- | ---: |
| Delivery | +8 |
| Road maintenance contribution | -2 |
| Exceptional contribution to maintaining contributions | -2 |
| Take-home pay | 4 |

“M” can become a central antagonist through the player's choices, but opposition is not mandatory. Accommodation, service, and private life under distant authority need developed outcomes too. Taxes should create situations and decisions, without making financial stability impossible.

## Story construction

Use authored arcs, conditional scenes, world facts, and delayed consequences. Branches may reconverge while preserving different relationships and world states. A declined letter must not merely remove content. Secrets belong to characters and letters, independently of Moro's traits.

Store immutable player events alongside versioned story content. Journal entries distinguish established knowledge from hidden world state. A summary must never reveal an undiscovered consequence.

## Still to author

Complete origins consistent with the shared motivation; trait effects; geography/cast beyond the opening; central mystery; complete act outline; each romance; political routes; private-life projects; endings and reboot variations. None of these is complete simply because the engine exists.


## Campaign direction — 2026-11-06

The user selected intertwined inhabitants' lives in the foreground, with a discreet mystery running through them. The five arcs aim to sustain approximately one real human year, superseding the earlier short campaign estimate. This is an authoring and pacing target, not a finished campaign or a requirement to wait for calendar dates.

Build recurring relationships, main quests, smaller quests, branching conversations and meaningful travel. Preserve complete private-life, non-romance, non-political and job-refusal routes. A return journey can revisit people and places changed by prior decisions. Each choice needs context-sensitive consequences, including modest everyday effects; severity must follow the situation.

The first work encounter must clearly offer employment, with dialogue choices before accepting the letter. Letters need authored outcomes for being kept sealed, opened, destroyed unread or destroyed after reading, plus witnesses and later effects where appropriate. An opening event cannot be erased by later destruction. Character truth, Moro's knowledge and other characters' knowledge must remain distinct.

Full plots, private character biographies, letter bodies, hidden conditions and endings must not be tracked in this repository. The owner selected a separate private manuscript repository and confirmed its initial push; new draft uploads and an independent backup remain separate checks. See [private authoring](narrative-authoring.md). Public documentation may describe mechanics and authoring requirements without spoilers. No full campaign manuscript is tracked here.

## Opening clarity review (November 7)

Each request should establish who asks, what Moro carries, whom or where to reach, whether it is paid, and what completion means. Literary letter titles may remain evocative; they cannot replace the practical instruction. The kettle request carries a message, not a kettle; the private opening offer now makes this explicit. Choosing a delivery selects its objective; walking direction is a separate compass decision. Deliberately withheld information must be framed as uncertainty within an otherwise intelligible task. The current opening manuscript was reviewed in both languages: the job, three topics, two legacy encounters/errands, eight recurring conversation sets, twenty request offers/details/reports and place introductions. Requests now name the people and destination, separate paid work from personal favors, and explain outward travel and optional returns. The deliberately unnamed destination explicitly withholds its meaning while giving delivery instructions. Relay presence now matches its actual mechanical sender. This does not author or validate the future five arcs.
